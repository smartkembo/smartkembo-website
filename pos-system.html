FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to buy a special barcode scanner?
No. Any smartphone camera works out of the box. A USB scanner or ESP32 station is optional, for shops that want a faster dedicated counter setup.
Can my staff see each other's sales?
No. Each cashier only sees their own sales and totals. Only the shop owner sees everything across all staff and stores.
What happens to stock when something sells?
It's deducted automatically the moment the sale is completed — no manual stock-taking needed. Low-stock alerts warn you before you run out.
Can I run more than one shop with one account?
Yes. Add as many stores as your plan allows, each with its own products, stock, and staff — all visible from one owner dashboard.
Can I track discounts my staff give?
Yes. Any time a cashier applies a discount, they must record why. You can review every discount and the reason given, per sale, per staff member.
What if I want to stop using it?
Simply stop paying the monthly fee. No contracts, no cancellation fees, no paperwork.
